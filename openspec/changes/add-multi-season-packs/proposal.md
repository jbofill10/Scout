# Change: Multi-season packs, and the search fixes they depend on

## Why
A back catalogue such as SpongeBob seasons 5-10 has no single-episode torrents on the indexers Scout uses; the only releases are whole-series packs ("S01-13 1080p COMPLETE"), which the season-pack search cannot read (it takes "S01-13" for season 1, episodes 1-13) and which would download thirteen seasons to get six. Three search defects found while diagnosing the 2026-10-30 failures compound this: a torrent queued behind qBittorrent's active-download limit is read as a dead swarm, deleted and blacklisted for good; a show with many TVDB aliases spends the whole search deadline on the preferred indexer so the fallback indexer is never asked; and show names are matched as substrings, so "Dragon Ball" accepted "Dragon Ball DAIMA" and filed sixteen of its episodes under the 1986 series.

## What Changes
- **Multi-season packs.** With `season_packs` on, a request spanning several seasons first searches for one pack covering all of them ("Show S05-S07", "Show complete series", "Show seasons 5-7", "Show complete"), then falls back to one pack per season with enough episodes, then per episode. Pack titles that name a season range ("S01-13", "Seasons 1-5", "S08 and S09") or a complete series are understood; a pack spanning more seasons than wanted still qualifies, scored below an exact one. `PackInfo` carries the seasons covered.
- **Pack file selection.** A pack is added paused. Once its file list is known in qBittorrent, files holding no wanted episode are deselected, the free-space guard is applied to the size of what is left, and the torrent is started; a pack whose wanted files still do not fit is removed and its episodes reported short of disk space. So a complete-series pack fetched for six seasons downloads six and is judged on six. Files are matched by their own numbering, with a bare episode number read against the season folder they sit in; the same matcher links the files when the pack lands. When a pack torrent is given up on, every episode it stood for is marked lost so the retry engine picks them all up.
- **State-aware dead-swarm check.** A torrent in `queuedDL`, a checking, allocating, moving or paused state is waited for: neither the dead-swarm grace nor the no-progress timeout runs. A torrent qBittorrent puts in an error state is given up on after the grace period without blacklisting the release.
- **Expiring exclusions.** A release given up on stays excluded for `FAILED_TORRENT_TTL` (default 14 days) instead of forever.
- **Shorter search ladder with a fair deadline.** Names are deduplicated by normalised form, "Title (Other title)" aliases are split, and at most four names are searched; the rare query forms ("1x05", "Season 01 Episode 05", "E05") are tried under the title only; repeated queries are dropped. The search deadline is shared out between the indexer tiers, unused time rolling over, so the fallback indexer always gets its share.
- **Word-level name matching.** A parsed title matches when its words equal the expected title's, or equal them once articles, years and country tags are set aside, or add one leading possessive ("Marvel's"). A title with an extra trailing word is another show. Upgrade searches carry the item's year.

## Impact
- Affected specs: `torrenter-downloads`
- Affected code: torrenter `models` (PackInfo.Seasons, FailedTorrent.FailedAt), `service/torrent_pack.go`, `service/mediaprocessor_pack.go`, `service/torrent_monitor.go`, `service/torrent_failures.go`, `service/torrent_search.go`, `service/torrent_strategy.go`, `service/torrent_validate.go`, `repository/postgres.go`, `interactors/upgrade_interactor.go`
- Settings: none added; multi-season packs follow `season_packs`. New env `FAILED_TORRENT_TTL`.
